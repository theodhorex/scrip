Scrip logo concepts

Eight marks on a 64 grid, one colour, drawn as outlined shapes. Each shows 120 px, 32 px, 16 px and reversed (dark on light). Working drafts for choosing a direction. Not checked for trademark conflicts.

01 Cap

A bar hovers over a block and the gap never closes: the budget line the agent cannot cross.

02 Doorway

A slab with one doorway: the tall light door from the hero, as a portal you pass through.

03 Gates

Three posts stepping down: checks in a row, each one narrowing what passes.

04 One tooth

A tall key with a single tooth: it opens request_payment and nothing else.

05 Geometric S

Two arcs, flat terminals: an S that cannot be misread as a 5 and has no swoosh.

06 Limit S

The S under a hard limit bar: the name and the rule in one mark.

07 Door S

A tall door slab with the S carved out as light: reads at 16 px and echoes the hero door.

08 Token

A scrip token with a door cut in it: a limited-use token, spendable only through the gate.
