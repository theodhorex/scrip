## PM Notes (Recognized Boundaries)

The corrections above align error CODES with real fail-closed behavior. Case families `KAT-B-05-125..138` (header spoofing) and `KAT-B-07-140..` (bursts) send dummy/empty bodies, so requests reject at body validation regardless of headers. Their PASS status proves "rejected", not "spoofed headers have no effect". Proving that requires a VALID delegate (which relays when headers are clean), adding the spoofed header, and verifying identical behavior; this is scheduled as follow-up task D2.
